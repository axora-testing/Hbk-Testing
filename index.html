Welcome to HBK & Associates LLP

A complete financial practice, built for how business actually works

HBK & Associates LLP is a professional firm offering a complete package of top-notch services covering all aspects of audit & assurance, taxation, corporate law and various entrepreneurial services — for businesses whether large or small.

Founded in 2011, we are a mid-sized chartered accountancy firm headquartered in Mumbai, serving clients across India and globally. Our partners combine technical expertise with a deep understanding of business realities, so advice lands as decisions rather than documents.

We support startups, alternative investment funds, NRI clients, offshore institutions and private equity — incorporations, registrations, accounting, tax and regulatory compliance, all under one roof.
